01 / THE PREMISE

Safety is a fragile thing.

They thought they were safe. They thought they were prepared.

In a nation teetering on the edge of chaos, a group of strangers is locked inside a remote military compound. What begins as a mission of protection quickly unravels into something far more dangerous—something spiritual.

As tensions rise and secrets come to light, the line between ally and enemy blurs. Loyalties are tested. Faith is shaken. And every decision carries weight, not just for survival, but for the soul.

“The real war isn’t just outside—it’s within.”Within These Walls · Book One

03 / THE AUTHORS

Jannell & Eric Turner

Within These Walls was written by my husband and I to explore that question through the lives of ordinary people facing extraordinary circumstances. The series follows believers as they endure government restrictions, martial law, imprisonment, betrayal, and the daily struggle to survive while holding on to their faith.

This isn’t just a story about end times. It’s a story about courage, conviction, family, sacrifice, and the question every believer must eventually answer:

“How much are you willing to endure for what you believe?”
